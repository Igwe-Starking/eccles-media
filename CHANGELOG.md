# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release: streaming JPEG and PNG decode/encode, WAV (PCM, IMA-ADPCM), MP3 playback through minimp3,
  AVI and MP4/3GP/MOV demux and mux, cooperative player, ESP-IDF port (playback, capture, recording).
- `ECCLES_MEDIA_VERSION_*` macros and a plain CMake target (`eccles::media`).
